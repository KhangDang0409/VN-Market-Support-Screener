RULE-BASED MARKET SCREENING

VN Market Support Screener

Screen Vietnamese stocks using historical price and volume data from Yahoo Finance. The model filters for liquidity and 60-session price amplitude, then checks for a double-bottom bounce, proximity to a volume-weighted price zone, or a high-volume breakout.

Built by Khang Dang.

How the screening rules work

Stocks need at least 120 downloaded rows. Average 20-session turnover must meet your liquidity threshold; the latest 60-session high-to-low amplitude must be at least 15%.

Double Bottom Bounce: the recent 15-session low must be within 2% of a historical closing-price valley; price must have bounced 2–6% and RSI must be below 50. Valleys use order-10 local minima and the original date exclusion.

POC Support: price must be between 98.5% and 103% of the POC, the recent 20-session high above 104%, and the recent 15-session low at least 97%. POC is the midpoint of the highest-volume bin in a 20-bin histogram of 60 closing prices; each day's volume is assigned to its closing-price bin.

Confirmed Breakout: price must reach the previous 60-session high, volume must exceed 1.2 times the 20-session average (including today), and RSI must exceed 60. Rules are checked in this order; the first qualifying setup wins.

RSI uses exponentially weighted gains and losses with alpha 1/14. Distance is absolute and truncated to an integer percent. Prices and turnover use provider values without unit or currency conversion.

Behind the project

I first built this tool as a terminal program in 2025 to reduce the time I spent checking individual tickers. I later turned it into a website so fellow traders could explore the same screening rules.

This project filters information; it does not provide investment advice. A qualifying setup is not a prediction or a guarantee of a profitable trade.
